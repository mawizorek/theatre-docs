# Paper tech

Walking every cue in the prompt book with the design team before anybody is
standing on stage waiting.

This page exists as the first real content on this site and as a demonstration:
it is a `procedure`, it names an owner, and the table above is drawn from its
frontmatter by the type rather than typed by hand.

!!! warning "The cost of skipping it"

    Every cue not agreed on paper gets agreed in the room instead, with the
    full company on the clock. It is the cheapest hour in the process and the
    easiest one to cut.

## Why it is general and not venue-specific

Nothing above names a building. That is the test for whether a page belongs on
this site: a venue's page can link here for the practice and add only what is
true of its own room.
